Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains when orders are cancelled and how refunds work on BuyEasy.

1. Automatic cancellation

If an order is not finished within 12 hours of being placed, BuyEasy cancels it automatically. Orders that were already picked up by a rider are not cancelled automatically, because the goods are on their way.

2. Wallet payments

If you paid with your BuyEasy wallet and your order is cancelled, the full amount is returned to your wallet. If the final delivery fee turns out lower than the amount held at checkout, the difference is returned to your wallet when a rider accepts your order.

3. Cash orders

If you chose cash on delivery and the order is cancelled, you have not paid anything, so there is nothing to refund.

4. Problems with a delivered order

If something is missing, wrong or damaged, message BuyEasy support on WhatsApp or from the Support button in your account within 24 hours of delivery. Please include your order number. We will speak to the store and rider and, where the problem is confirmed, may credit your wallet or arrange a fix.

5. Declined orders

If a store declines your order, it is cancelled and any wallet payment is returned to you.

6. Withdrawals

Wallet withdrawals are not available yet. We will update this page when payments and payouts are switched on.
