FAQ

Questions people ask

Where do you deliver?

BuyEasy is launching in Nkomazi, Mpumalanga. You will see stores within 15 km of your saved delivery location.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery starts at R10 and adds R1.50 for each extra: every kilometre after the first, bad weather, evenings (18:00 to 05:00) and Sundays. You see the delivery fee at checkout, and if it ends up lower you only pay the lower amount.

How do I pay?

Pay cash to the rider when your order arrives, or use your BuyEasy wallet. Card payments are coming soon.

What if my order does not arrive?

If an order is not completed within 12 hours it is cancelled automatically and any wallet payment is refunded. You can also message our support team at any time.

How do I sell on BuyEasy?

Create an account, apply to become a store, and wait for our approval. Once approved you can add products and go online.

How do I become a rider?

Create an account and apply as a rider. After approval, ask the stores you want to work for. When a store approves you, its orders appear in your Jobs tab.

Is my information safe?

We only collect what we need to run your orders. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
