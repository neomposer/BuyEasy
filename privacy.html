Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

BuyEasy ("we", "us") is a local shopping and delivery marketplace serving Nkomazi, Mpumalanga. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: email address, name and phone number.
  • Delivery details: your delivery address and the map pin you choose.
  • Orders: what you ordered, the store, payment method, status and amounts.
  • Store and rider applications: the details you give us so we can review and approve you.
  • Ratings, comments, feedback and support messages you send us.
  • Usage information: an anonymous visitor ID so we can count visits, and the time stores and riders were last active.
  • Location during deliveries: a rider's phone location is used on the rider's own device to show the route while a delivery is active.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account, process orders and arrange delivery.
  • To let stores, riders and buyers contact each other about an order.
  • To handle payments, refunds and wallet balances.
  • To provide customer support and review complaints and ratings.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the platform safe, including suspending accounts when needed.
  • To understand how BuyEasy is used and improve it.

3. Who sees your information

  • The store and rider on your order see your name, phone number and delivery address so they can complete it.
  • Service providers that run BuyEasy for us, such as our hosting and database provider, and our payment provider once card payments are switched on.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as long as we need it for orders, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it where the law allows. You can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. Contact us below and we will respond as soon as we can.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No online service is completely risk free, so please keep your password private.

7. Children

BuyEasy is not meant for children under 18 to use without a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
