  • You are responsible for activity on your account.

3. Orders and payment

  • You can pay cash on delivery or with your BuyEasy wallet. Card payments will be added later.
  • The delivery fee starts at R10 and adds R1.50 for each extra: every kilometre after the first, bad weather, evenings (18:00 to 05:00) and Sundays. The fee is shown at checkout. If the final fee is lower, you only pay the lower amount.
  • Stores within 15 km of your delivery location can deliver to you.
  • For cash orders, please have the exact amount ready for the rider.

4. Cancellations

Orders that are not completed within 12 hours of being placed are cancelled automatically. See our Refunds & cancellations page.
